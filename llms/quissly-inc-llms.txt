# Quissly

> AI-powered search and chat platform for e-commerce websites.

Quissly helps online stores deliver intelligent product discovery through semantic AI search and a conversational shopping assistant called QChat. It replaces traditional keyword-based search with AI that understands natural language queries like "something to wear to a beach wedding" and maps them to relevant products.

## Products

### QSearch
AI-powered product search engine for e-commerce. Features include:
- Semantic search that understands shopper intent
- Natural language query understanding
- Visual similarity search
- Automatic synonym and typo handling
- Multi-language support (40+ languages)
- AI product recommendations
- Custom merchandising rules
- Voice search
- Real-time analytics dashboard

### QChat
Conversational AI shopping assistant. Features include:
- Natural language product discovery through dialogue
- Personalized product recommendations
- Product question answering
- Guided shopping experiences
- CRM and messaging integrations
- Admin panel for management

## Pricing

QSearch and QChat are priced separately (no combo/bundle). QSearch is metered by
search requests and QChat by AI interactions (chat messages); monthly sessions are
only a guide to help merchants self-identify. Overage is progressive (cheaper per
unit at higher tiers) and billed as you go — there is no forced tier jump. Monthly
prices shown below; annual billing is cheaper.

### QSearch Plans
- Free: $0 — 1,000 AI searches/mo (hard cap)
- Starter: $39/mo — 10,000 search requests/mo (best for ~5,000 monthly sessions); overage $3 / 1,000
- Growth: $89/mo — 50,000 search requests/mo (best for ~25,000 monthly sessions, most popular); overage $2 / 1,000
- Scale: $229/mo — 200,000 search requests/mo (best for ~100,000 monthly sessions); overage $1 / 1,000
- Enterprise: Custom — 100K+ / multi-store

### QChat Plans
QChat includes human-in-the-loop, omnichannel & live chat support across all tiers.
- Basic: $50/mo — no AI; omnichannel + live chat human support (no interaction meter, no overage)
- Starter: $119/mo — 5,000 AI interactions/mo (best for ~5,000 monthly sessions); overage $12 / 1,000
- Growth: $279/mo — 20,000 AI interactions/mo (best for ~25,000 monthly sessions, most popular); overage $8 / 1,000
- Scale: $799/mo — 100,000 AI interactions/mo (best for ~100,000 monthly sessions); overage $5 / 1,000
- Enterprise: Custom — 100K+ / multi-store
